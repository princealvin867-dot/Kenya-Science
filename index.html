Department of Information Technology

The Department of Information Technology (IT) in a university manages and supports the institution’s computer systems, networks, software, databases, and digital services. It helps students and staff use technology effectively for teaching, learning, research, and administration.

  • Hands-on learning in modern IT labs
  • Support for digital innovation and research
  • Strong focus on practical, career-ready skills
